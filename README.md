# 華童患瘋症（《東華報》1925-08-08 第7版，澳洲國家圖書館） / Hua tong huan feng zheng [A Chinese Child Sent to Hospital]: Transcription Dataset

《東華報》1925-08-08 刊載的〈華童患瘋症〉。雪梨《東華報》記一名華人留學少年身體不適，經延醫診察後送列度灣流行病醫院療治。正文另有一欄原題。

Hua tong huan feng zheng [A Chinese Child Sent to Hospital] appeared in Tung Wah Times (1925-08-08). This local report describes a twelve-year-old Chinese student who became unwell in Sydney. After a physician examined him, the newspaper says he was sent to hospital at Little Bay.
